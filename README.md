# xi-observable

Two instruments for measuring emergence in dynamical systems: **Ξ** (emergence achieved, built on synergy/redundancy decompositions over a tetrad S = (D, V, C, μ)) and **Γ** (emergence in formation, a capability-formation probe).

The central result was imposed by the experiments rather than assumed: the "becoming" of a structure splits into three measurable phases. A later audit found a circularity in the coarse-graining step, so Ξ is now treated as a template rather than a scalar measure; the neutrality test is in the repo.

Start here (French): `BILAN.md` (plain-language overview, every term defined), `SYNTHESE_VULGARISEE.md`, `FINDINGS_XI_V2.md`, `PAPER_OUTLINE.md`, and `theory/` for the formal side.

Part of a research program on emergence alongside [emergetics](https://github.com/zacccarie/emergetics), [architrace](https://github.com/zacccarie/architrace) and [dynamoscope](https://github.com/zacccarie/dynamoscope).
